Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 29, 2026 • Canonical URL: https://bibleunlock.in/privacy

At Bible Unlock, we believe that your spiritual walk, personal prayers, and reading habits are sacred. Our software is architected with a strict zero data collection and offline-first philosophy.

1. Local On-Device Data Storage

All reading progress, completed chapters, personal prayer requests, bookmarks, verse collections, reading streaks, and customized app blocking settings are stored exclusively on your local device using high-performance encrypted on-device storage.

We do not maintain remote user databases, tracking pixels, or third-party analytics servers that record what verses you read, when you pray, or which apps you choose to shield.

2. Mobile Operating System Permissions

To deliver mechanical distraction defense, Bible Unlock requests specific native operating system permissions:

  • Apple iOS (FamilyControls & Screen Time API): Used strictly on your iPhone or iPad to shield and unshield selected apps based on your daily reading timer. Apple's privacy architecture ensures that Bible Unlock cannot see what you do inside those apps or access any personal device data.
  • Android (Accessibility Service & Usage Access): Used exclusively on your Android device to detect when a user-selected distracting app is opened in the foreground and display the Bible Unlock shield overlay. This service does not log keystrokes, intercept screen content, or transmit telemetry over the internet.

3. Subscriptions & In-App Purchases

Subscriptions for the Sanctuary tier are processed securely through Apple App Store (In-App Purchases) and Google Play Billing via RevenueCat. Bible Unlock never collects, handles, or stores your credit card details, billing address, or payment credentials. RevenueCat assigns an anonymous random device identifier strictly to verify active entitlement status without personal profiling.

4. Third-Party Services & Advertising

Bible Unlock is 100% ad-free. We do not sell, rent, monetize, or share any personal data with data brokers, advertising networks, or third-party marketing platforms.
