# Brag Plan: Bible Unlock

## What is this app?
Bible Unlock is a Christian digital discipline mobile app that blocks distracting phone apps until the user completes their daily Bible reading goal.

## The angle
"Your Bible is the key that unlocks your phone." 
A cinematic, sacred craftsman launch film contrasting the daily guilt of doomscrolling with the mechanical discipline and peace of putting God's Word first every morning.

## Hook (first 2-3 seconds)
A high-contrast cinematic shot of a phone trying to open social media, immediately slammed with a radiant gold sacred padlock:
`LOCKED UNTIL YOU READ 📖`
Voice / Text: *"You told yourself you'd read the Bible today. Then you opened Instagram."*

## Key moments (the middle)
1. **The Lockdown:** Social feeds are frosted over; the timer counts down. Focus is enforced.
2. **The Sacred Reader:** Elegant serif Scripture (*Matthew 11:28*), 92 translations, quiet reverence.
3. **The Unlocking Ritual:** Reading goal met; radiant golden burst and haptic chime unshields the phone.
4. **The Daily Momentum:** 14-day streak with Grace Day shield and 336 visual scripture cards.

## Outro / punchline
The golden shield crest locks into place:
`BIBLE UNLOCK`
`Block Distraction. Unlock With Scripture.`
`bibleunlock.in`

## User flow worth showing
1. **Attempted distraction:** App shield blocks Instagram.
2. **Sacred devotion:** User reads for 10 minutes in Bible Unlock.
3. **Spiritual victory:** Holy golden unlock burst unshields apps for the day.

## Tone
- Preset: `cinematic`
- Creative direction: Sacred craftsman product film
- Interpretation: Dramatic contrast, deliberate pacing, gold radiant lighting, bold typographic confidence.

## Format: landscape — 1920x1080 (also adaptable for 1080x1920 vertical)
## Duration: 18.0 seconds

## Visual identity (from the project)
- Background: `#070b09` (Celestial Dark)
- Accent: `#f5b800` (Radiant Sacred Gold)
- Success: `#5db872` (Emerald Grace)
- Text: `#faf9f5` (Sacred White)
- Display font: `EBGaramond` (Serif Display)
- Body font: `Inter` (Sans Bold/Medium)
- Strongest visual element: The Radiant Sacred Padlock with starfield vignette overlay.

## Share copy (draft)
Your Bible is now the literal key that unlocks your phone. Stop scrolling, start reading with Bible Unlock: bibleunlock.in

## Audio direction
- Role: Cinematic support with low ambient swell and crisp mechanical unlock hit
- Music: Cinematic ambient bed (`happy-beats-business-moves-vol-12-by-ende-dot-app.mp3`)
- Music treatment: Low atmospheric opening, swelling rhythm through the reading flow, celebratory peak at unlock (13.5s), gentle fade under logo.
- SFX posture: Restrained, high tactile value: phone lock click, gentle page turn, golden chime burst, haptic confirmation.

## Storyboard

### Scene 1 — The Trap (0.0s – 3.5s)
- **Visual:** Dark cinematic vignette. A smartphone mockup shows social icons glowing faintly, then abruptly freezes behind a frosted dark glass shield with a bold radiant gold padlock.
- **Headline (Inter Bold):** `YOU TOLD YOURSELF YOU'D READ TODAY.`
- **Sub-badge (Gold):** `[ SHIELD ACTIVE // APPS LOCKED ]`
- **Audio intent:** Low ambient drone, subtle glitch/lock thud.
- **Transition mood:** Hard dramatic cut → Scene 2.

### Scene 2 — The Key (3.5s – 8.0s)
- **Visual:** Smooth zoom into the Bible Reader. Sacred typography renders Matthew 11:28 in EB Garamond with golden progress arc counting down: `10:00 → 00:00`.
- **Headline (EB Garamond):** `“Come to me, all who labor and are heavy laden...”`
- **Sub-badge:** `92 TRANSLATIONS • SACRED FOCUS`
- **Audio intent:** Warm harmonic swell, soft ambient peace.
- **Transition mood:** Warm glow wipe → Scene 3.

### Scene 3 — The Victory (8.0s – 13.5s)
- **Visual:** The timer hits zero. A golden burst shockwave radiates outward. Status changes to `SACRED GOAL ACHIEVED`. Social apps unshield with green checkmarks.
- **Headline (Inter Bold):** `READING GOAL MET. APPS UNLOCKED.`
- **Telemetry Card:** `14-DAY STREAK ACTIVE 🔥 • GRACE DAY READY`
- **Audio intent:** Bright golden chime, uplifting percussive beat drop.
- **Transition mood:** Smooth cinematic zoom → Scene 4.

### Scene 4 — The Outro (13.5s – 18.0s)
- **Visual:** Clean centered branding. The Bible Unlock green-and-gold shield icon glints in 3D center.
- **Headline (EB Garamond):** `Bible Unlock`
- **Tagline (Inter 600):** `Put God Before The Scroll.`
- **URL pill:** `bibleunlock.in`
- **Audio intent:** Sustained warm chord, gentle decay to silence.
