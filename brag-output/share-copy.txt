Most screen-time blockers fight human nature with brute force—and fail. You get a pop-up, hit "Ignore for 15 minutes", and keep doomscrolling.

We took a completely different angle with Bible Unlock:
Instead of punishing digital habits, we redeem them.

Before your distracting apps open, Bible Unlock presents a thoughtfully curated verse of Scripture. You reflect, read, and reset your attention before deciding whether to proceed.

Key principles we built around:
1. Friction that inspires rather than frustrates.
2. 100% on-device privacy (Screen Time API & FamilyControls).
3. Beautiful, distraction-free typography with zero ads.

## 3. Reddit (r/Christianity / r/OpenChristian / r/productivity)
**Title:** I built an app that replaces screen time passcodes with Scripture reading

Hey everyone,

Like many of you, my screen time on social apps (Reddit, YouTube, Instagram) was getting out of hand. Typical app blockers never worked for me because clicking "15 more minutes" or typing a numeric passcode is purely subconscious.

I wanted something that interrupted the dopamine loop with something that actually grounds me.

- Clean typography (KJV, ESV, NIV, etc.)
- 100% private, on-device blocking
- No guilt, no shame—just gentle mindfulness
